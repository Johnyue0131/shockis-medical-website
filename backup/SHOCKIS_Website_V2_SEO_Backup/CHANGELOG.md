# Version History

## v0.1.0 — 2026-08-13

First working version of the Aven Medical international website framework.

### Included

- Responsive React and Tailwind website foundation.
- Homepage with brand positioning, product categories, quality credentials, and international-market messaging.
- Product portfolio page covering patient monitoring, respiratory care, surgical solutions, and point-of-care devices.
- FDA and compliance page explaining classification, evidence, submission, and lifecycle management.
- Contact page with commercial details and an enquiry-form interface.
- Shared navigation, footer, page hero, product visuals, and mobile menu components.
- Site-wide metadata and responsive styles for desktop, tablet, and mobile layouts.
- Vinext, Vite, and Cloudflare-compatible production build configuration.

### Current limitations

- Product information and contact details are demonstration content and must be replaced with verified company data before launch.
- The enquiry form is presentational and is not connected to an email service, CRM, or database.
- Product visuals are CSS illustrations rather than final product photography.
- Product-specific regulatory status must be verified before publishing commercial claims.
